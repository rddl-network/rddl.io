Regulatory Requirements

SPV Manager must be AIF-compliant (AIFMD)

IN WORK

AIF Compliance

SPV pools investor capital into real assets (BESS). Under EU law, this qualifies as an Alternative Investment Fund. The SPV manager must be AIFM-registered or use a licensed AIFM partner.

SOLVED

Settlement Layer

EUR stablecoin (EURAU) is already a legal E-Money Token under MiCA. Handles investment inflows, yield distribution, and O&M payments on-chain.

SOLVED

Crypto Trading Partner

Licensed Handelspartner exists for fiat-crypto conversion. Investors can on/off-ramp between EUR and EURAU through a regulated gateway.

OPEN

KYC at Scale

Onboarding a broad investor base requires scalable KYC/AML infrastructure. Operationally expensive for retail investors. Solution: partner with licensed platform or limit to qualified investors initially.

No Security Token Required

The base model distributes real energy market revenue — no variable-value token, no prospectus requirement, no MiFID II licensing. Security Token is an optional future layer for secondary market liquidity.

Trust Layer: RDDL Network

Notarized production data from the asset itself provides the auditable proof that investors, regulators, and auditors need. Every kWh is cryptographically verified — not self-reported.
